Solcrest by Bricks & Milestones is located off Hennur Road in Bengaluru, Karnataka. The project site is situated at Sy No. 25/1, Bileshivale Main Road, Rampura Village, Hobli, Hennur, Bidarahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka – 562149.
Solcrest is developed by Bricks & Milestones, a Bengaluru-based real estate developer known for design-focused residential communities. Solcrest is one of the developer's ongoing residential projects in Northeast Bengaluru.
Solcrest offers 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 3.5 BHK and 4 BHK apartments. The residences range from approximately 1,332 sq. ft. to 2,164 sq. ft., providing spacious layouts designed for contemporary urban living.
Solcrest is spread across 9.3 acres and features 6 towers with 796 residences, rising up to 33 storeys. The project offers around 90% open spaces, spacious curved balconies, only 4 units per floor, no common walls, vehicle-free zones, co-working spaces, walking trails, sports facilities, a swimming pool, kids' play areas and a 30,000 sq. ft. clubhouse.
